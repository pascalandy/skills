# chef-triage

You have the final say on findings and when reviews end.

1. **Decide.** Accept or reject each finding, with a one-line reason. Before your first decision in step 2, wait for both manic-1 and manic-2, then decide on both sets together, with the findings of your premortem from `chef-launch`. On a later pass, wait only for the passes you delegated on the same heads. A watch wake brings findings too: a failed check or a conflict is an accepted finding for the Cook, and an outside review is decided like a barrage finding. Done when every finding has a decision and a reason
2. **Relay.** Send each accepted code finding to Cook <b>.<c> with `t3_thread_send`. Give the accepted docs findings to a fresh DocWriter with `chef-launch`: section Fix when the stack has a docs PR, section Update otherwise. Done when each owner reports the findings pushed to their layers, or why they could not be
3. **Next.** Each barrage counts its own passes against its `max-passes` in `config.toml`, and a barrage whose review ended keeps its decisions. If fixes need a fresh look and passes remain, delegate a fresh pass of that barrage on the new heads with `chef-launch`, carrying its prior rounds. Otherwise end that review and record why for the closing report. When the last allowed pass yields a code fix, delegate one more pass, limited to that fix. That pass is the last: list any fix it yields under "What you couldn't confirm" in the closing report. Done when the next pass is delegated, or the review ended with its reason recorded
