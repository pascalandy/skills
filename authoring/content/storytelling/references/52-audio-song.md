# Audio and song

## Podcast and audio narrative

Use voice, proximity, sonic texture, silence, editing, music, auditory orientation, and
movement between narration and recorded material.

**Central diagnostic**

Does sound create meaning rather than merely accompany text?

## Song

Use speaker, prosody, refrain, motif, compression, transformed repetition, sound
pattern, emotional progression, and the relation between lyric and music.

**Central diagnostic**

Does a returning phrase change in meaning or intensity?
