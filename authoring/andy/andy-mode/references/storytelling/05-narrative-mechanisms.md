# Narrative mechanisms

A narrative mechanism is a repeatable causal relation between a craft choice and an
operation the audience performs. It is not a theme, structure, trope, style adjective,
or desired effect by itself.

Keep the terms distinct:

- An **engine** is the continuing source of forward movement, such as an inquiry,
  negotiation, ritual, accumulation, or objective under pressure
- A **mechanism** explains how the telling invites an audience operation
- A **device** is the observable, often medium-specific choice that realizes it
- An **effect** is the resulting change in attention, understanding, anticipation,
  feeling, participation, or memory
- A **structure** arranges mechanisms and changes across a larger span

Use this chain:

```text
craft choice -> perceptible cue -> audience operation -> state shift -> story function
```

Treat the chain as a hypothesis about audience experience, not a psychological fact.
The cue must be observable in the work. The state shift must serve the audience promise.

## Select by need

1. Name the audience's entry state and intended exit state
2. Identify the missing operation, such as anticipating, inferring, comparing,
   revaluing, choosing, remembering, or feeling pressure
3. Choose the smallest mechanism set that can cause that operation
4. Realize each mechanism through a capability of the target medium
5. State the failure condition, cost, or tradeoff
6. Test the choice by removing, delaying, replacing, or reordering its cue

Combine mechanisms only when they perform different jobs. A pile of techniques is not
movement.

Mechanisms may form a causal sequence. The audience may recognize a pattern, anticipate
its return, notice a violation, then revalue earlier units. At another scale, one cue
may perform several jobs. Describe the actual chain instead of counting mechanisms.

When two mechanisms compete, let the audience promise decide. Explicit explanation may
improve orientation while destroying inference. Surprise may intensify a turn while
weakening causal credibility. Keep the tradeoff visible.

## Mechanism map

Use the map as routing vocabulary, not a checklist. A telling may depend on one family
or combine several.

### Orient a situation model

- **Audience operation**: locate the relevant time, space, attention carrier, goal,
  condition, or change and update that model as the telling moves
- **Craft choices**: concrete anchor, entrance, transition, repeated landmark,
  viewpoint cue, goal cue, spatial relation, or selective recap
- **Failure condition**: orientation explains what the audience already tracks, or
  withheld anchors prevent the audience from forming the question the work needs

### Make value perceptible

- **Audience operation**: recognize what matters and why a possible change, loss,
  discovery, or relation deserves attention
- **Craft choices**: specific desire, protected routine, act of care, valued object,
  contrast, commitment, vulnerability, competence, cost, or revealing choice
- **Failure condition**: the telling declares importance without a perceptible basis,
  or demands emotion before the audience understands the value at risk

### Open a prospective gap

- **Audience operation**: ask a live question, form an expectation, or anticipate an
  event
- **Craft choices**: promise, question, deadline, absence, interrupted action,
  incomplete pattern, or delayed answer
- **Failure condition**: withholding removes orientation or promises an answer the work
  never intends to address

### Distribute knowledge

- **Audience operation**: compare who knows what, infer hidden information, or revise an
  earlier model
- **Craft choices**: restricted access, concealment, reveal, dramatic irony,
  misreading, recognition, or recontextualization
- **Failure condition**: obscurity substitutes for a meaningful information gap, or a
  reveal adds data without changing prior meaning

### Bind cause to consequence

- **Audience operation**: understand why a state changed and project what an action may
  produce
- **Craft choices**: setup and payoff, action and reaction, tactic and result, choice
  and consequence, feedback, or cascading effects
- **Failure condition**: coincidence replaces agency, or the causal chain becomes so
  explicit that the audience has nothing left to infer

### Put value under pressure

- **Audience operation**: discover what matters by observing a cost, refusal, tactic,
  compromise, or contradiction
- **Craft choices**: obstacle, tradeoff, scarcity, dilemma, negotiation, status move,
  sacrifice, or incompatible goods
- **Failure condition**: conflict is manufactured only to add intensity, or suffering
  is used as a shortcut to importance

### Control narrative time

- **Audience operation**: notice duration, sequence, recurrence, simultaneity, or the
  changed meaning of an event
- **Craft choices**: scene, summary, ellipsis, dilation, compression, delay, reorder,
  repetition, return, or parallel action
- **Failure condition**: chronology is rearranged without affecting understanding,
  anticipation, rhythm, or meaning

### Control access and proximity

- **Audience operation**: align with, observe, distrust, or gain distance from an
  attention carrier
- **Craft choices**: focalization, point of view, narrative distance, withheld
  interiority, direct address, unreliable account, or change of scale
- **Failure condition**: access changes arbitrarily, or intimacy is assumed merely
  because the telling uses first person or close detail

### Make the audience infer

- **Audience operation**: connect cues, read subtext, or supply what the work omits
- **Craft choices**: gap between speech and action, silence, omission, displaced
  emotion, image, gesture, negative space, or off-screen event
- **Failure condition**: the needed cues are absent, or explanation repeats the
  inference after the audience has already made it

### Establish and transform a pattern

- **Audience operation**: recognize recurrence, compare versions, and assign new
  meaning to a return
- **Craft choices**: motif, refrain, echo, ritual, parallel, accumulation, contrast, or
  repetition with variation
- **Failure condition**: repetition returns unchanged without intentional stasis, or a
  motif decorates the work without changing interpretation

### Shape and violate expectation

- **Audience operation**: predict from a pattern, then confirm, complicate, or replace
  that prediction
- **Craft choices**: convention, setup, foreshadowing, misdirection, reversal,
  surprise, anti-climax, or delayed fulfillment
- **Failure condition**: surprise depends on hidden rules, or fulfillment is so exact
  that the work becomes inert

### Let relationships move

- **Audience operation**: track changes in trust, status, intimacy, obligation, or
  power
- **Craft choices**: offer and response, request and refusal, tactic and countertactic,
  disclosure, withheld recognition, alliance, betrayal, or changed terms of address
- **Failure condition**: dialogue exchanges information while the relationship remains
  mechanically unchanged

### Make form carry meaning

- **Audience operation**: experience a relation through rhythm, image, sound, body,
  space, layout, or interaction before or beyond explanation
- **Craft choices**: syntax, cadence, composition, edit, silence, page turn, gutter,
  staging, sonic return, spatial relation, or interface behavior
- **Failure condition**: form merely ornaments content, or a device imported from
  another medium loses the function it once performed

### Make participation consequential

- **Audience operation**: choose, explore, perform, remember, or recognize personal
  implication
- **Craft choices**: branching, state change, rule, feedback, constrained choice,
  audience address, participation, or delayed consequence
- **Failure condition**: the action only uncovers fixed material and cannot affect
  interpretation, relation, possibility, or state

### Create closure or residue

- **Audience operation**: integrate what changed, revalue the beginning, or carry a
  live question beyond the ending
- **Craft choices**: payoff, recognition, transformed return, decisive image, release,
  deliberate omission, unresolved obligation, or reframing
- **Failure condition**: explanation closes an effect already carried by action, or
  ambiguity excuses an ending that makes no final move

## Diagnose a mechanism

When a passage fails, locate the broken link in the causal chain:

- The craft choice may be absent, mistimed, or redundant
- The cue may be imperceptible in the target medium
- The audience may lack the context required to perform the operation
- The operation may occur but produce the wrong shift
- The shift may be real but unrelated to the audience promise

Repair the earliest broken link. Do not intensify every later device to compensate for
an absent premise, cause, cue, or audience reason.

## Transfer across media

Transfer the function, not the source device. A prose omission may become off-screen
space in film, a gutter in comics, silence in audio, an unavailable action in a game,
or a withheld demonstration step in an explanation. Verify that the target device
causes the same audience operation under the new medium's conditions.

## Final mechanism check

For each major mechanism, be able to answer:

- What audience operation is needed here?
- Which observable cue invites it?
- Why should that operation produce the intended shift?
- Which medium capability realizes it?
- What would become weaker, clearer, or different if the cue changed?
- When would this mechanism become confusing, manipulative, inert, or false?
