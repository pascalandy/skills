## 11. Final audience test

After the story, can the audience answer:

- Why does this exist?
- Who is it for?
- What situation produced it?
- Why this approach?
- How does it work at the needed level?
- What changes after using it?
- Which value claims are supported, inferred, or unknown?
- Where does it not fit?
