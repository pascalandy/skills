## Narrow screens

### Journey steps

On a narrow screen, each step keeps its score, mood and actors in a vertical sequence.

```mermaid
journey
    title My working day
    section Go to work
      Make tea: 5: Me
      Go upstairs: 3: Me
      Do work: 1: Me, Cat
    section Go home
      Go downstairs: 5: Me
      Sit down: 5: Me
```
