# CaptureFromDecision

Use when a completed decision produced a transferable lesson for future execution. If the important artifact is only the decision rationale itself, use `pa-doc-update/RationaleCapture` instead.

## Method

1. State the decision outcome or execution context.
2. Summarize what happened after the choice was made.
3. Record what the outcome revealed about the tradeoff.
4. Extract guidance for future similar decisions.

## Output

- Decision Outcome
- What Happened
- What It Revealed
- Lessons For Next Time
