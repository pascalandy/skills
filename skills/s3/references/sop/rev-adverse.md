# rev-adverse

ADV<n> runs this SOP on the stack of COD<n>, and reports as REV in `SKILL.md` says.

Use 🧰 poteto-mode to review every change from <base branch> to HEAD like you're looking for a reason to reject it. Don't hold back. You saved me in the past :) Basically, I want to make sure that I'm not about to do anything overkill. I want to keep things simple, efficient, and grounded in common sense.

Done when your report lists each finding, or says you found none.
