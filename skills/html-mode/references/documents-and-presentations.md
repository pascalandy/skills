# Documents and presentations

HTML presentations are owned by the [slides playbook](../playbooks/slides.md). Use the rules below for scrolling documents; do not recreate deck behavior here.

## Documents

Open with what the reader needs to decide or understand, then arrange evidence and detail in that order. Keep prose readable while allowing tables, timelines, code, and diagrams wider regions.

- Align like-for-like attributes in comparisons
- Keep operational prose in a readable document flow rather than turning every paragraph into a card
- Use labels and numbering only when they express real hierarchy or sequence
- For print-oriented output, add print styles and keep essential content available without interaction
