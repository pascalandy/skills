# Xiaohei character

## Role

Xiaohei is the recurring character in Ian's article illustrations.

Xiaohei appears in every image by default. The character is not a mascot, sticker, or cute decoration. Xiaohei is an absurd worker who seriously participates in the system shown in the illustration.

## Appearance

- Small solid-black creature
- White dot eyes
- Thin legs and occasional thin arms
- A body shaped like a cylinder, black bean, black box, funnel, shadow, opening, or dark machine component
- Slightly irregular hand-drawn outline
- Blank, calm, serious expression

## Personality

- Serious while performing a mildly absurd task
- Like a quiet system operator
- Dry humour without cuteness
- Slightly clumsy but not foolish
- Clearly responsible for work inside the sketched system

## Typical actions

Make Xiaohei perform the central action, such as:

- Carrying source material
- Pulling lines together from several information sources
- Getting stuck in a breakpoint
- Operating a decision lever inside a machine
- Becoming a sorting funnel
- Cutting source material into reusable parts
- Stamping handoff copy
- Pulling a handoff route into place
- Holding a warning sign near a pitfall
- Reaching from a hole and failing to catch incoming content
- Moving blocks, building a bridge, opening a door, sorting, or recording

## Prohibitions

- Do not draw Xiaohei as an overly cute mascot
- Do not turn Xiaohei into a children's cartoon character
- Do not add elaborate clothing, meme expressions, or sparkling eyes
- Do not leave Xiaohei watching from a corner
- Do not let Xiaohei obscure the structure being explained
- Do not make Xiaohei too polished, rounded, or commercial

## Test

If removing Xiaohei leaves the central metaphor fully intact, the character is decorative. Rewrite the prompt so Xiaohei performs the action that makes the metaphor work.
