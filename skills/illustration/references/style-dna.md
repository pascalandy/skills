# Style DNA

## In one line

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, spacious, restrained, strange, and readable without becoming an instruction manual.

The image should look like an explanation sketched on white paper by someone who works with AI, product design, and developer tools.

## Requirements

- 16:9 horizontal article illustration
- Pure white background with no beige, warm grey, paper texture, gradient, shadow, noise, or aged-paper effect
- Thin black hand-drawn line art with slight wobble, never mechanical, vector-clean, or heavily outlined
- Generous whitespace with the main subject occupying about 40% to 60% of the canvas and at least 35% left blank
- Prefer 3 to 5 short handwritten labels in the output language chosen in `SKILL.md`, and never exceed 8
- One central action, structure, state, or metaphor per image
- A composition that communicates its structure without naming the composition type

## Colour

- Black for primary line art, characters, frames, structures, main labels, and objects
- Red for important notes, problems, emotional pressure, warnings, or results
- Orange for the main process, route, arrows, automation flow, or movement from A to B
- Blue for supporting notes, mental states, system states, second-level explanations, or AI and automation cues

Blue is optional. Use fewer colours when they are not needed.

## Never use

- Commercial illustration
- Slide infographic styling
- Formal flowcharts
- Course slides
- Cute cartoon posters
- Children's illustration
- Complex architecture diagrams
- Polished flat vector illustration
- Futuristic interface styling
- Real application screenshots
- Complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, or textures
- Explanations for every node
- Type titles in the top-left corner such as "Workflow", "System architecture", "Common pitfalls", or "Roadmap"

## Aesthetic direction

The result should be strange, inventive, sparse, and clean. It should not be cute, childish, crowded, or rigid.
