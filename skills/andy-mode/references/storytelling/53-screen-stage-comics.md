# Screen, stage, and comics

## Film and television

Use visible behavior, image, space, sound, editing, gaze, off-screen space, sequence,
and contrast between what is said and shown.

**Central diagnostic**

Does information exist dramatically without depending on literary explanation?

## Theatre

Use body, presence, shared space, playable action, silence, entrance, exit, address,
and the relation between text and performance.

**Central diagnostic**

Can each important intention become action or presence on stage?

## Comics

Use panel, strip, page, gutter, omission, composition, text-image relation, page turn,
and spatial rhythm.

**Central diagnostic**

Does the sequence use what the audience constructs between images?
