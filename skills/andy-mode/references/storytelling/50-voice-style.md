# Voice and style

Load this reference only to preserve an existing voice, define a style, or use an
artistic reference. Medium-specific decisions belong in the matching medium reference.

## Style dimensions

Describe style through observable dimensions such as:

- dense or spare
- realistic or stylized
- intimate or distant
- linear or fragmented
- clear or ambiguous
- fast or contemplative
- grave or light
- explicit or implicit
- explained or discovered
- direct or restrained
- closed or open
- harmonious or dissonant
- plot-led or presence-led
- narrator present or effaced

Treat these as ranges, not binary switches.

## Voiceprint

To preserve an existing voice, observe:

- sentence length and shape
- vocabulary and register
- rhythm
- density of imagery
- how emotion is named
- relationship to humor
- degree of explanation
- distance from people or subject
- use of silence and omission
- expressive irregularities

Do not correct an irregularity merely because it is nonstandard.

## Artistic references

For a reference guiding the current deliverable, separate:

- surface realization such as syntax, diction, composition, texture, or cadence
- narrative mechanism such as information distribution, transformed repetition,
  relational movement, visual omission, or polyphony
- the effect the user values
- the conditions under which the effect belongs in this assignment

Read [narrative mechanisms](05-narrative-mechanisms.md) when the reference affects how
the audience anticipates, infers, revalues, chooses, or tracks change.

Use a reference to extract a mechanism, not to imitate an identity.

If the user asks to incorporate the lesson into storytelling's general guidance, return to
`playbooks/storytelling.md` and route to **Evolve**. Do not turn a current-use style profile into a
permanent rule without the evolution admission gate.
