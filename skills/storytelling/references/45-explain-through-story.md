# Explain through story

Use this branch to give primarily nonnarrative source material a truthful narrative
form.

Do not decorate an explanation with an anecdote. Reconstruct the causal story the
subject actually supports.

Choose one reconstruction reference:

- For a natural phenomenon, concept, historical development, social process, or other
  subject with no designed intervention, read
  [concept or process reconstruction](46-explain-concept.md)
- For a designed artifact, project, product, service, architecture, research artifact,
  proposal, or operating process, read
  [artifact reconstruction](47-explain-artifact.md)

When the subject is hybrid, choose according to the audience's main learning job.
Load the other reconstruction only if a consequential claim cannot be handled without
it.

**Completion criterion**

One reconstruction reference is selected from the nature of the subject and audience
job. No creator, intention, design insight, user, or value bridge is assumed for a
subject that does not have one.
