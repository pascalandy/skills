# Sharpen

Before starting, ask yourself how a top expert would approach this task: their raw sources, their real methods, and an outside check that the result is right. Ask yourself what the user is really after, and whether the task is over- or underspecified. Loosen constraints that don't serve the goal as readily as you add ones that do, and leave choices that are the user's to make open. Fold in only what makes the work better, not bigger, and always keep one outside check that the result is right. Then do the work itself, without narrating the framing or handing back a rewritten prompt.
