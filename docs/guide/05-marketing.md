# Do marketing with `corey-mode`

`corey-mode` holds 50 marketing playbooks by Corey Haines, copied with credit. In this page you write your product context first, then use it to write a page, find ideas, ask a panel of famous marketers, and write emails.

## Write your product context first with `product-marketing`

```text
marketing ; product-marketing. I run a two-person bookkeeping service for restaurants in Montreal.
```

The agent walks you through one section at a time: what you sell, who buys it, their problems, your competitors, the objections you hear, and your tone. If you have a website, give the agent its address. The agent drafts the whole document from your pages, and you correct the draft.

The result is a product context document. Every other marketing playbook reads it first, so you stop explaining your business in every chat. A chat app can't save it for later chats, so keep it yourself:

- Add it to a project's files or instructions, so every chat in that project sees it
- Or paste it at the start of a marketing chat

## Write a page with `copywriting`

```text
marketing ; copywriting. Write the homepage for my bookkeeping service. Our clients are restaurant owners.
```

You get the page section by section: the headline, the subheadline, the call to action, and the body of each section. The agent explains its key choices and offers two or three options for the headline and the call to action.

## Find ideas when you're stuck with `marketing-ideas`

```text
marketing ; marketing-ideas. I have no marketing budget. What should I try first?
```

The agent suggests ideas that fit your stage and budget, before you choose a channel.

## Ask a panel of experts with `marketing-council`

```text
marketing ; marketing-council. Should I lower my prices to win more clients?
```

The agent answers as a board of famous marketers, such as Seth Godin, David Ogilvy, and April Dunford. Each one weighs in from their own point of view, so you see where they agree and where they don't.

## Write emails with `emails` and `cold-email`

```text
marketing ; cold-email. Write a first email to restaurant owners about my bookkeeping service.
```

Use `cold-email` for people who don't know you yet. Use `emails` for sequences sent to people who signed up, such as a welcome series.

## Find the other routes

The 50 routes fall into seven families: foundation and strategy, SEO and site, conversion, copy and content, paid and measurement, growth and retention, and sales and go-to-market. [The skills list](../references/remote-skills-general.md) names each one. You can also ask:

```text
Which marketing route fits a launch announcement for a new service?
```

A few routes connect to outside tools, such as ad platforms or analytics. In a chat app, they give you the plan and the copy, and you do the clicks.

**Pitfall:** the word "marketing" starts `corey-mode`, even in a sentence about something else. To keep another skill in charge, name it: `andy-mode ; storytelling. Tell my marketing career story.` When a request names another mode, that mode keeps the task.
